# LinkedIn Post — English

How do you choose the next retail location when the best-looking site is not
necessarily the best portfolio addition?

I built a geospatial decision-support platform for a fictional Istanbul retailer that
connects microzone demand, network accessibility and commercial economics to
constrained expansion planning.

Highlights:

- 5,965 H3 microzones and 24 candidate locations
- 5/10/15-minute drive and walk catchments
- competitor, POI, white-space and cannibalization analysis
- spatial block CV demand model with SHAP explanations
- auditable AHP factor contributions and weight sensitivity
- budget, distance and capacity constrained maximum-coverage optimization
- FastAPI, PostGIS, Excel, PBIP, interactive maps, PowerPoint and governance PDF

In the synthetic base scenario, four sites use TRY 93.4m and add 1.33 million people
to modeled 10-minute coverage. The more important result is the method: each score,
assumption, warning and constraint can be inspected.

No personal data. No invented dashboard KPIs. No investment claim. Every result comes
from the reproducible pipeline and remains subject to field and human review.
