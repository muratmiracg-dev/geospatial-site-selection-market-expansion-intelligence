# LinkedIn Project Description — English

**Murat Miraç Gedik — Independent Data Analyst & Business Intelligence Developer**

Built an end-to-end **Geospatial Site Selection & Market Expansion Intelligence**
platform for a fictional retail chain operating across metropolitan Istanbul.

The project turns candidate-site analysis into an auditable portfolio decision:
5/10/15-minute drive and walk catchments, H3 microzone demand, competitor and POI
density, white-space opportunity, Huff/gravity customer diversion, cannibalization,
spatially validated sales potential, SHAP explanations, AHP scoring, weight sensitivity
and budget-constrained maximum-coverage optimization.

The verified synthetic pipeline evaluates 5,965 H3 cells and ranks 24 candidates. The
base scenario selects four complementary sites within TRY 93.4m, adding 1.33 million
people to modeled 10-minute catchments and raising total modeled market coverage to
23.1%. Demand validation uses five spatial blocks/folds and produces out-of-fold MAE
of TRY 3.82m and R² of 0.964; these metrics describe a deterministic synthetic
benchmark, not real investment performance.

The delivery includes a modular Python/GeoPandas/Shapely/H3 package, PostGIS model,
FastAPI, Folium maps, formula-driven Excel, PBIP starter, executive PowerPoint,
methodology/governance PDF, Docker/Kubernetes, Prometheus/Grafana, tests, CI, CodeQL,
dependency/container security and operating governance.

All commercial outcomes are synthetic, no personal data is used, and every output
remains subject to human, field and commercial review.
